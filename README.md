One sentence, at most 200 characters, on a small moment of wonder in everyday life:

> The librarian stamps my book at 4:58, and the moth that lives in the lampshade lands on the date stamp, wings still warm.

`main.ts` copies `entry.txt` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`).

Run: `node main.ts`
